The fonts are each under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1. Each is cut
down to the Latin characters and to the weights and settings these pages
use, and sent as WOFF2; that makes each a modified version, released under

scenes of the app, and a browser fetches one only when a scene sets text
in it.

preview /og-v2.png, are of Open Matte itself and of its printed look, made
for this project; the words in them are drawn in the faces above and in one
more under the same licence, Grape Nuts (the staff pick's hand), of which no
file is sent. The film in them, Dr. Strangelove (1964), is only named: no
poster, still or title lettering of the film appears.

loads the moving scenes of the app only as they come near the screen; those
scenes bring the code below with them.
