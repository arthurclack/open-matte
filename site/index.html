How a ticket reaches a friend.

Pick a film

Publish a film you’ve prepared, with your few lines on why it’s worth their evening.

Sherlock Jr.

Buster Keaton, 1924

Send a ticket

An admit-one link, with an optional screening limit, start-by date and days after first play. Revoke it and it stops at the next request.

The Open Matte admin, on a film’s page: a ticket for anyone with the link, one screening, valid for 7 days after first play, has just been issued. Under the form: 1 ticket ready, for Sam, with its link and Copy link pressed, now saying Copied.

They press Play

Any current browser will do: phones, TVs and apps’ built-in browsers included. They need no account.

48 hours

by default, to pick up where they left off, on one device at a time.

Not a media server.

No library of everything you own. You prepare each film, and the server sends it as it is, never transcoding.

Your server, their browser.

38 GB

at most, for a two-hour 4K HDR film with every rendition. Often much less.

Yours

A Linux server
Docker and Docker Compose, a public IPv4 address, ports 80 and 443 open.
A domain
Caddy gets its certificate.
Prepared films
Encoded on your computer with scripts/encode.mjs (Node and ffmpeg).
A TMDB key
Optional, for details and artwork.

Theirs

A browser
No account needed.
A passkey
Only if you give them an account.
